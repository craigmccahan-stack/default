## 3. AI: tailwind vs. headwind

**Airo**
- Airo and Airo AI Builder have been merged into one platform [S3].
- The annualized bookings run-rate reached **$50M**, up 5x from the $10M disclosed one quarter earlier, and the growth was nearly all organic [S3].
- Management (Bhutani and CFO McCaffrey) declined to give pricing or unit economics, calling it "too early" [S27].
- Management named these headwinds [S3]:
  - Airo absorbing legacy products is moderating A&C bookings.
  - Some costs are shifting into H2.
  - AI compute costs.
  - LLM-driven changes in the domain market.

**Agent identity (ANS)**
- GoDaddy ANS launched in April 2026 as the first public implementation of the open ANS standard, which anchors agent identity in DNS and PKI. The standard is IETF draft "ANS v2", and LegalZoom publishes its MCP-server agent through ANS [S28].
- In Q2-26 GoDaddy said it intends to contribute ANS to the Linux Foundation. It is co-developing Agentic Resource Discovery (ARD) with Cisco, Google, Microsoft, Nvidia, Salesforce and others, and launched the GoDaddy Developer Platform [S3].

**.ai domains:** more than 1M registrations industry-wide, about 20x in 5.5 years (Feb-26) [S53].

**Management's AI framing:** Bhutani said GoDaddy held a "consistent share" of the domains market. He acknowledged that LLMs are changing how customers discover businesses and search, but said domain funnel economics, traffic and conversion were healthy in Q2 [S3].

**Bear evidence**
- **AI Overviews:** Wells Fargo says they now appear on 48% of searches [S29].
- **Zero-click searches:** 68.01% of U.S. Google searches in Jan–Apr 2026, up from 60.45% in 2024 (SparkToro/Similarweb) [S52].
- **Click-through rates (Pew):** 8% when an AI Overview appears vs. 15% without one, and only 1% click a link inside the Overview [S52].
- **Wix:** Q2-26 bookings $569M (+12%) and revenue $563M (+15%). Its Base44 vibe-coding product reached $150M ARR by early May 2026 [S49].
- **Lovable:** ARR went from $300M (Feb-26) to $400M (Mar-26) to $500M (Jun-26), and it raised $400M at a $13.3B valuation [S50].
- **Hostinger:** bundles its AI builder with hosting and a free domain [S51].
- **Scale gap:** Airo's $50M run-rate is small next to these competitors.
- **Sell-side read after Q2:** analysts cited Wix's AI site-builder competition and Airo "not yet driving growth fast enough" [S47b].

## 4. Capital allocation

**Buyback authorization**
- The $3.0B authorization approved in April 2025 runs through 2027.
- Remaining: $2,165.2M at 12/31/25 [S6] and **$1,331.6M at 6/30/26** [S23]. That implies about $834M was used in H1-26 (d).
- At $98, the remaining authorization buys about 13.6M shares, or ~10.7% of diluted shares [calc].

**Prior $4.0B program (May-21 to 2025):** completed. It bought 43.7M shares at an average of $91.45, a gross reduction of more than 25% in fully diluted shares since Jan-22 [S17, S59].

**Multi-year framework**
- The 2024–26 targets were $4.5B+ of cumulative FCF and a 33% NEBITDA margin by 2026 [S38].
- GoDaddy is on track: about $1.36B + $1.61B + $1.8B (guide) ≈ $4.8B (d).
- New targets come at **Investor Night, 12/1/26 in Tempe**. The agenda covers strategy, the "AI-first transformation" and refreshing select financial targets [S39].
- FCF/share, share-count and leverage targets: n/f.

**Debt**
