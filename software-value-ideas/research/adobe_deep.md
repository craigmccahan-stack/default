## 3. AI-disruption debate

**Threat evidence**
- **Google:**
  - Nano Banana launched Aug 2025 and Nano Banana Pro Nov 2025 ([Klue](https://klue.com/newsletter/nano-banana-kills-photoshop)).
  - Veo 3.1 Lite launched 3/31/26 at $0.05/sec ([Oakgen](https://oakgen.ai/blog/state-of-ai-creative-tools-2026)).
  - "Google Pics", an AI image editor built into Workspace, reached general availability in 2026 ([Tech-Insider](https://tech-insider.org/?p=21062)).
- **OpenAI:** Sora 2 came out in 2025. gpt-image-2 launched 4/21/26 with ~100% text rendering and 2K output ([Oakgen](https://oakgen.ai/blog/state-of-ai-creative-tools-2026)).
- **App downloads:** by early Oct 2025, Gemini downloads were up 331% while Firefly's were down 68% ([Cryptopolitan](https://www.cryptopolitan.com/google-nano-banana-snatched-adobe-users/)).
- **Midjourney:** V1 video model launched 6/18/25 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/18/midjourney-launches-its-first-ai-video-generation-model-v1/)). Michael Burry urged Adobe to buy Midjourney on 3/6/26 ([DayTraders](https://daytraders.com/news/2026/03/06/michael-burry-urges-adobe-to-buy-ai-firm-midjourney-to-save-creative-moat)).
- **Software sell-off:** a Feb 2026 software sell-off was blamed on Anthropic's Claude Cowork ([Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/06/24/should-you-buy-adobe-after-its-40-drop/), 6/24/26). ADBE fell from a ~$423 52-week high to ~$241 ([Techleap](https://finder.techleap.nl/news/feed/adobe-stock-plummets-to-241-as-ai-fears-trigger-software-sell-off)).
- **Canva:**
  - **ARR:** about $4B, growing about 35% per COO Obrecht ([SaaStr](https://www.saastr.com/canva-crosses-a-stunning-4b-arr-but-what-would-it-be-worth-today)). Another source says +43% from $2.8B.

**Adobe's responses**
- **Firefly hosts 30+ models.** These include Google's Nano Banana 2 and Veo 3.1, OpenAI, Runway Gen-4.5 and Kling. Custom Models are in public beta ([Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/adobe-new-ai-capabilities-and-custom-models-expand-fireflys-video-and-image-creation-platform)).
- **Firefly Foundry** builds custom enterprise models on a brand's own IP. It launched 10/20/25 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/20/adobe-launches-a-foundry-service-that-builds-custom-generative-ai-models-for-enterprises)).
- **MAX 2025** (LA, 10/28–30/25):
  - Firefly Image Model 5 (native 4MP).
  - Generate Soundtrack and Generate Speech.
  - Agentic assistants in Photoshop and Express.
  - Partner models from BFL, Google, Luma, OpenAI, Runway, ElevenLabs and Topaz ([Adobe PR](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/adobe-expands-creative-possibility-ai-every-creator-adobe-max-2025-2025-10-28)).

  - All Apps became Creative Cloud Pro. The annual plan billed monthly went from $59.99 to $69.99, prepaid annual from $659.99 to $779.99, and month-to-month from $89.99 to $104.99.
  - The plan includes 4,000 premium generative credits a month, unlimited Generative Fill and Firefly Boards ([ProVideo Coalition](https://www.provideocoalition.com/please-read-adobes-official-article-on-the-pricing-changes-coming-to-the-creative-cloud-plans/), 2025).

## 6. Valuation
- **Current multiples [d]:**
  - 9.7x the FY26 guide midpoint EPS ($24.475).
  - 8.7–9.1x FY27 consensus EPS ($26.26–27.49).
  - EV is about $93.4B. That gives 9.5x FY25 FCF ($9.85B), 8.6x TTM OCF ($10.81B) and 8.3x FY27E FCF ($11.23B).
  - The FY25 FCF yield is 10.6%. The remaining $24.55B buyback is 26% of market cap.
- **Forward P/E:**
  - 15.2x on 8/6/26, against 19.5x a year earlier ([Eulerpool](https://eulerpool.com/stock/Adobe-Stock-US00724F1012/forwardpe)).
  - Offside Research says ADBE is at a "new all-time low forward P/E". Since 2000 it has traded below 20x forward P/E in only 11 of 67 quarters, and 9 of those 11 were followed by positive 1-year returns ([Offside](https://offside-research.beehiiv.com/p/adobe-the-ai-trade-nobody-is-talking-about)).
- **Historical averages (trailing P/E, not forward):** 5-year 41.4x and 10-year 44.4x ([FullRatio](https://fullratio.com/stocks/nasdaq-adbe/pe-ratio), acc.). **5- and 10-year average forward P/E and EV/FCF: n/f.**
- **2022 trough:**
  - About 24.8x forward EPS and 9x forward sales, described as "near 5-year lows" ([TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/news/article/adobe-nasdaqadbe-stock-looks-appealing-after-its-51-downfall), ~Q4 2022).
  - The stock hit a ~3-year low after the 9/2022 Figma deal ([Futurum](https://futurumgroup.com/insights/adobes-stock-got-slammed-for-spending-20-billion-on-figma-but-it-now-owns-a-rare-company/)).
