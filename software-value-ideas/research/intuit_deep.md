**Notes on the table:**
- **FCF definition:** FCF here is OCF minus PP&E purchases, which matches your $6.12B for FY25. Capitalized software was $46M in FY26, $40M in FY25 and $59M in FY24; deducting it as well puts FY26 FCF at about $8.62B (d).
- **FCF margin (d):** 29–34% from FY19 to FY25, and 40.5% in FY26, which the tax benefit lifted.
- **SBC as a share of revenue (d):** 5.9% in FY19, rising to 11.9% in FY23–24, then about 9.6% in FY26. The FY27 guide of $2.02B is about 8.6%.
- **Share count (d):** diluted shares rose 5% from FY19 to FY26, even though Intuit bought back about $15.5B of stock over FY20–26. The likely cause is shares issued to pay for Credit Karma and Mailchimp [bk].
- **New non-GAAP basis:** from FY27, non-GAAP figures include SBC. On that basis the FY26 margin was about 32.1%, and FY27 is guided to about 34.6% ([Quartr](https://quartr.com/events/intuit-intu-q4-2026_3sXlcGJO)).

- Online Ecosystem paying customers grew 5% in FY25.

- **Takeaway:** growth is coming mainly from price and mix, not new customers.

- Total online payment volume, including Bill Pay, grew 30% to more than $225B in FY26.
- QuickBooks Capital loan volume was $1.9B in Q4 FY26, up 54%.

**Big Bets:** Intuit's "Big Bets" (assisted tax, money and mid-market) were 30% of FY26 revenue and grew 34% (Q4 script).

**TurboTax Live (assisted tax):**
- FY23: revenue +17%, customers +12%.
- FY24: revenue +17%, customers +11%.
- FY25: revenue +47% to $2.0B (41% of Consumer revenue); customers +24% (FY23–25 releases).
- FY26: revenue up about 36–37% to roughly $2.8B; customers up 38%; about 53% of TurboTax revenue ([Nasdaq/Zacks](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/intuits-turbotax-live-thriving-can-it-offset-diy-choppiness), Aug 2026).

**Pricing:**
- On the 8/25/26 call, Intuit said: "Price is now the number one reason customers leave TurboTax." It also said it lost "quality DIY customers" to lower-cost providers ([Quartz, 8/26/26](https://qz.com/intuit-turbotax-customers-price-revenue-guidance-082626)).
- **FY27 plan:** lower entry-level prices, lower first-year tax revenue per customer, and wider distribution.
- **Free Credit Karma Tax:** federal filing is $0 and state filing is $15 ([Credit Karma](https://www.creditkarma.com/about/releases/free-credit-karma-tax-file-federal-return-for-free)).

**IRS Direct File:** not offered in the 2026 filing season, with "no launch date set" ([dontmesswithtaxes](https://dontmesswithtaxes.com/direct-file-is-officially-dead/), late 2025). It handled 140,803 returns in 2024 and 296,531 in 2025, under 0.5% of all returns ([SageNext](https://www.thesagenext.com/blog/irs-direct-file-shutdown-free-alternatives-cpas)).

**IRS 2026 filing season, through 5/8/26** ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/filing-season-statistics-for-week-ending-may-8-2026)):
- 144.99M returns received.
- Self-prepared e-files: 65.73M, up 1.7% year on year.
- E-files by tax professionals: 75.32M. They were down 2.3% as of 4/10 ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/filing-season-statistics-for-week-ending-april-10-2026)).
- The average refund was up about 11%.

**Rough share estimate (d):** TurboTax units divided by IRS self-prepared e-files were about 61.7% in 2025 and 59.3% in 2026, a loss of about 2.4 points. The cutoff dates do not match: the IRS counts run to 5/8, while TurboTax counts the season to 7/31. Intuit had already expected to lose about 1 point of total IRS returns in 2025 ([FY25 Q3 press release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000089687825000020/fy25q3earningspressrelease.htm)).

**AI tax competitors:**
- **Goldman Sachs (6/2/26)** ([Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/goldman-sachs-downgrades-intuit-ai-104654463.html)):
  - Says Prime Meridian, Perplexity Tax and Chime Tax are "credible, scalable products".
  - Puts the AI cost of a standard return at about $0.12, against TurboTax's average revenue of about $162 per return.
  - Base case: if 20% of filers move fully to AI by 2030, TurboTax revenue ends up about 18% below FY25.
  - One secondary summary says Goldman's FY28 EPS estimate is 13% below consensus ([Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/news/feed/goldman-slashes-intuit-to-sell-cuts-target-to-276-as-ai-tax-rivals-threaten-turbotax-dominance)). Verify this.
- **Perplexity "Computer for Taxes"** launched around 4/2/26 ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/taxes/2026/perplexity-launches-ai-agent-that-drafts-tax-returns/)). It drafts a federal return on IRS forms and needs a Pro subscription.
- **Prime Meridian** launched free AI-native filing on 3/5/26 ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260305141195/en/Prime-Meridian-Launches-Free-AI-Native-Tax-Filing-Platform-Designed-to-Eliminate-Hidden-Fees)). It raised $3.5M led by General Catalyst in January 2026 ([Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/prime-meridian-a74b)).
- **Chime** has offered free in-app filing through april and Column Tax since January 2025 ([Chime](https://investors.chime.com/news-releases/news-release-details/chime-offers-literally-and-truly-100-free-app-tax-filing)).
- **Robinhood** launched a tax suite on 2/18/26 ([Robinhood](https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/robinhood-takes-on-tax-season-with-a-new-suite-of-services)).
- **Evidence of traction:** I found no user or return counts for any of these entrants. The best indirect evidence is TurboTax losing DIY share.

## 4. Restructuring, FY27 guidance and Investor Day targets

**Workforce cut:**
- The [8-K filed 5/20/26](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000896878/000089687826000024/intu-20260520.htm) covers a cut of about 17% of staff, roughly 3,000 people (the WSJ says about 3,100).
  - Charges of $300–340M, mostly in Q4 FY26.
  - Substantially complete by 10/31/26.
- **Annualized savings: not disclosed.** "Intuit didn't provide a projection of expected long-term savings." The savings will fund the Big Bets and improve margins ([WSJ via MarketScreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/intuit-to-cut-17-of-staff-invest-in-big-bets-update-ce7f5ad9df80ff27)).

**FY27 guidance (8/25/26):**
- Non-GAAP operating income, now including SBC, of $8.063–8.145B, up 17–18%. That includes about $2.02B of SBC ([CFO Dive](https://www.cfodive.com/news/intuit-expects-2027-revenue-decelerate-cfo-AI/828869/)).
- That implies a margin of about 34.6%, against 32.1% in FY26.
- GAAP operating margin is guided to about 32%, up 440 basis points ([Quartr](https://quartr.com/events/intuit-inc-intu-investor-day-2026_3sXlNN79)).

**Investor Day (9/17/26):**
- Q1 and FY27 guidance reaffirmed ([Finviz](https://finviz.com/news/393199/intuit-hosts-investor-day-reaffirms-first-quarter-and-fiscal-2027-guidance)).
- The 3-year revenue growth (CAGR) target for the business platform is now 10–15%, down from 15–20% a year earlier ([TIKR](https://www.tikr.com/blog/intuit-slashed-its-long-term-guidance-heres-where-the-stock-could-go-from-here)). Consumer is 4–8%.
- **EPS growth target:** "at least high-teens" annual non-GAAP EPS growth ([BriefGlance](https://briefglance.com/companies/intuit-inc/pulses/79639)). Another summary said "mid-teens", so check the [transcript](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/intu/transcripts/612260-investor-day-2026/).
- FY26 FCF was $8.6B, and $6.8B (about 80%) went back to shareholders.
- **Segment conflict:** the presentation puts the business platform at $11.6B, which does not match reported GBS revenue of $12.9B. Reconcile it with the deck.

**AI metrics** ([MarketBeat](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/event-intuit-investor-day-puts-ai-at-center-of-growth-reset-after-customer-misses-2026-09-17/)):
- 2.5M customers use Intuit Intelligence automations and insights every month.
- Over 75% of IES customers use AI agents every month.
- Customers on the AI-native experiences get paid 4 days faster and do 30% less manual work.
- Agent Studio lets accountants build and deploy their own agents.
- New cheaper products for price-sensitive customers: QuickBooks Free/Lite and Credit Karma Tax.
- For "done-for-you" metrics, I found only one proxy: TurboTax Live customers grew 38%.
