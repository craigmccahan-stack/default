AI search kills the small-business website

Zero-click Google searches hit 68% in early 2026, up from 60% in 2024; AI Overviews show on 48% of searches. Yet the .com/.net base grew 5.1% (Verisign) and GoDaddy domains revenue +5%.

The website add-on is at risk; the domain itself is not.

AI builders take new customers

Lovable reached $500M ARR and Wix’s Base44 $150M, against Airo’s $50M run-rate. GoDaddy’s customer count is flat at 20.5M.

Real share loss at the top of the funnel; growth now comes from revenue per customer.

Promotions show pricing power is gone

The $4.99 .com offer cut bookings growth to 3–6%; revenue per customer still +9% and EBITDA margin 33%.

A self-inflicted bookings hit, not a margin problem.

Too much debt for a shrinking business

Net debt $2.7B, ~1.7x EBITDA; 2026 FCF ~$1.8B; $600M of notes due Dec 2027.

Comfortable: FCF covers net debt in 1.5 years.

Net: AI threatens GoDaddy’s add-ons more than its domains, and at a 10% owner yield slow growth is already priced.

Sources: SparkToro/Similarweb via Search Engine Land; Wells Fargo; Verisign Q2-26; Wix Q2-26; Lovable; GoDaddy filings.
