Generative AI commoditises creative tools

Revenue +13% and ARR +11% in Q3. Firefly now hosts 30+ outside models, including Google’s and OpenAI’s.

Adobe is the workflow layer, and is already selling AI on top.

Canva and Figma take the market

Both grow faster (Canva ~35%, Figma ~39%), but Adobe added ~$2.8B of revenue this year, ~70% of Canva’s entire ARR.

A bigger market, not a zero-sum one.

Net new ARR −36% means growth is rolling over

Driven by the freemium push and deferred price rises; total ARR still +11%, RPO +8%. Q4 is the seasonal peak.

The metric to watch. The FY27 guide is the test.

Leadership turmoil

CFO left in June; CEO Narayen hands over on 1 Dec to Anil Chakravarthy, ex-CEO of Informatica.

Risk of a kitchen-sink guide; also a chance to reset.

Net: two of the four bear arguments are contradicted by reported numbers; the other two get tested by the FY27 guide in December.
