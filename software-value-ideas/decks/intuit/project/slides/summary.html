Investment summary

A best-in-class platform at a distressed multiple

Our thesis

1

The engine is QuickBooks, not TurboTax. The business platform is 54% of revenue and grew 18%; QBO Accounting +23%.

2

TurboTax shrinks in units, not dollars. Units −2% to 39.0M, revenue +7%; assisted TurboTax Live is 53% of it.

3

Margins are stepping up. A 17% workforce cut lifts the FY27 GAAP operating margin to ~32%, up 440bp.

4

Priced for 1.8% growth forever, against guidance of 9–10% revenue growth and high-teens EPS growth.

Long, half size; add below $300. Base $400 (+23%); bear $210 (−36%).
