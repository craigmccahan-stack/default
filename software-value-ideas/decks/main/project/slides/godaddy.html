Idea 3 · GoDaddy (GDDY) · $107 · Long, half size

GoDaddy: a 10% owner cash yield on a domain toll road

The debate

Bears say AI search and AI site builders shrink small-business web presence. A .com promotion hurt bookings; RBC downgraded on AI risk (12 Oct).

Our view

1

Domains are identity. Businesses, and now AI agents, need them; the .com/.net base grew 5% in Q2.

2

Cash flow is still rising. About $1.8B of 2026 FCF is guided, up ~12%; that covers net debt in 1.5 years.

3

Buybacks, plus a possible bid. Buybacks retire ~13% of shares a year; Gen Digital made an unsolicited approach (FT, 24 Sep).

We are wrong if

Bookings keep lagging revenue into 2027, or AI builders take the website and marketing add-ons that drive margins.
