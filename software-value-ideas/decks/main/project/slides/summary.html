Summary

Three software cash machines priced for almost no growth

The sell-off over-reached. Software fell 37% peak to trough on AI fears; the rebound since April favoured AI winners, not cash-rich laggards.

We screen on owner cash. Free cash flow after stock comp, plus durability against AI agents.

Three names pass both tests yet price in under 2% growth a year forever, while growing revenue 6–10%.

Two more are close. FactSet and Salesforce price in ~4% growth; we would buy lower.
